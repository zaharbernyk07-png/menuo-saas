Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan at any time?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate your billing accordingly.
What happens if I exceed my staff limit?
We'll notify you when you're approaching your limit. You can either upgrade your plan or remove inactive staff members.
Is there a free trial?
Yes! All new accounts get a 14-day free trial of the Professional plan. No credit card required to start.
What are Giga and Conversion integrations?
Giga and Conversion are email marketing services that help you send automated newsletters and promotional emails to your customers about menu updates, events, and special offers.
